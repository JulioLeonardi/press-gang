Tiers are company-level, not role-level. “Known H-1B employer” means the company filed H-1B petitions in the USCIS FY2022–23 data — it is not a promise about this particular req. Sponsoring employers still post cleared and US-persons-only roles. Review state is saved in this browser only.

This board is wider than your Discord alerts. It includes the sponsorship unverified tier, which allow_unknown_sponsorship: false keeps out of the channel. Rebuilt once a day, each morning around 7 AM ET, from the live feeds — so a posting that disappears here has been closed or filled upstream.

Day headings are the source's posting date, not the build date. A posting the source published no date for is filed under Undated rather than being dated to the day the page happened to be built — about a third of the EU feed has no date on it. Undated does not mean old.
